Why renewal price matters

A ₹99 .in domain that renews at ₹899 costs ₹1,897 over three years. A ₹399 one that renews at ₹849 costs ₹2,097, while a flat ₹550 renewal comes to ₹1,650. The bar on each row shows how far the renewal price jumps from the first year.
