Privacy and affiliate disclosure

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Affiliate links

Some “Get deal” buttons on theintld.in are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission from the registrar at no extra cost to you. A partner may be listed first, and it is labelled “Partner”. The other rows follow the sort order you choose.

Price accuracy

We check each registrar’s public pricing page for India and update the list by hand. The date at the top of the page shows when we last did. Promotions, coupons and taxes change often, so please confirm the final price at checkout. If you find a wrong price, email us and we will fix it.

What we collect

We don’t ask you to create an account or enter personal details. The site uses a free third-party visitor counter that stores an anonymous visit count. Your browser keeps two small items on your device: the date of your last counted visit and your light or dark theme choice. You can clear them in your browser settings.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and its DNS is managed by Cloudflare. These services may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under their own policies.

Links to other sites

When you click a registrar link, you leave theintld.in. The registrar and its affiliate network may set cookies and have their own privacy policies.
